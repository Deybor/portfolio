# Amara: collection reasoning and next decisions

Research checked 30 September 2026. This is a public-catalogue review supporting an independent design proposal. It is not a claim to have accessed ÌTURA's sales, suppliers or production pipeline.

## Why extend Amara?

ÌTURA includes Amara Heart Necklace in its [bestseller collection](https://iturajewelry.com/collections/bestsellers). This is a useful brand-selected demand signal, not unit sales or proof that Amara outsells every other piece. The [necklace product page](https://iturajewelry.com/products/amara-heart-necklace) lists 316L steel and CZ. Its displayed retail price was ₦59,000 at review time; a retail price is not a manufacturing cost.

The [earring collection](https://iturajewelry.com/collections/earrings) already contains heart earrings, studs and an ear-stack set. The opportunity is an Amara-specific companion, not a claim that the brand has no studs. Whether its buyers want that extension still needs customer feedback and internal sales analysis.

[Mejuri's best-selling earrings](https://mejuri.com/collections/best-selling-earrings) include small gemstone studs and huggies. [Missoma's stacking guide](https://us.missoma.com/blogs/the-chain/how-to-stack-style-your-earrings) presents small earrings as a foundation for combinations with larger pieces. These are competitor merchandising and styling signals. They do not establish what ÌTURA's customers buy. The design inference is to explore a compact heart earring that works as a pair or as a starting piece in a stack; physical suitability remains a sample decision.

## Recommendation and alternatives

Lead with the Amara companion stud as a testable extension of an existing necklace. The drop expresses movement; the classic stud preserves the most direct resemblance; the folded stud introduces a distinct sculptural silhouette around the heart. This is the owner's design judgment, informed by the public research, rather than an assertion that the alternatives were commercially tested.

A simpler companion stud or a layering chain are subsequent candidates, not missing products to declare absent. Rank them using sales by design, Amara order combinations, stock availability, returns, repeat purchases and customer requests. A sold-out listing by itself cannot establish strong demand.

## Supplier stage and cost

Current evidence: a digital design and technical sheet. No confirmed supplier, accepted quote, manufacturing order or physical sample was supplied for this portfolio pass. Do not put invented supplier names, lead times, order quantities or unit costs on the website.

The technical proposal uses silver, vermeil and moissanite, a different material proposition from the necklace. Compare that route with a specification closer to the original before setting a retail target. Retain the approved CAD and technical sheet while discussing material selection as proposed.

[Formlabs' jewellery workflow](https://formlabs.com/eu/industries/jewelry/) distinguishes try-on prototypes, casting patterns and masters. [Cooksongold's casting FAQs](https://help.cooksongold.com/kb/en/faqs-363791) identify finishing and post manufacture as separate questions to resolve. These services are process references, not engaged suppliers or quotes for this design.

Request comparable quotes for the same revision, material, stone, finish, quantity and delivery destination. Separate one-time tooling/sample fees from recurring metal, stone, findings, setting, finish, packaging, freight and reject allowance. Use the resulting landed unit cost to assess a retail margin; no numeric cost is defensible before the specification and quotation are agreed.

Proposed Notion record: design/revision, parent collection, reason, evidence links, material options, supplier, sample status, quote and currency, quantity/MOQ, lead time, unit cost, tooling cost, target retail/margin, next decision and owner. This is a proposed working method; no Notion database was created or populated.
